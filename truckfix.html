🛡️ STOP & CHECK — Electrical Safety

🔥 Burning smell or smokeStop safely and isolate the electrical supply. Do not continue driving with an electrical fire risk.
🧩 Repeated fuse failureNever replace a fuse with foil or an oversized fuse. Find the cause of the overload.
🔋 Battery workKeep sparks and flames away from the battery and use appropriate protective equipment.

✅ Before You Continue Driving

Use this quick visual checklist after an electrical repair.

  • ✓ Lights and indicators work correctly.
  • ✓ No burning smell, smoke or exposed wire is visible.
  • ✓ Battery terminals and major connections are secure.
  • ✓ No fuse has been bypassed with wire or foil.
  • ✓ The truck starts normally and the dashboard shows no unexpected warning.
  • ✓ A serious electrical fault has been checked by a qualified mechanic.
